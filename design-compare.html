Colour sets, light and dark

Contrast is measured against the background. Text and links need 4.5 to be comfortably readable; green passes, red fails. For the screenshot palettes the roles were assigned by rule: lightest colour as light background, darkest as dark background, the opposite end as text, the most saturated readable colour as links.
